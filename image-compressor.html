Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compress a photo for a UK visa application for free?
Yes. ZenithCraft's Image Compressor works entirely in your browser — no upload, no signup. Set your target file size in KB and download instantly. Trusted by users in the UK, USA, Australia, Canada and UAE.
Will compressing an image reduce its quality?
You control the quality. Use the quality slider to find the right balance. At 70–80% quality, most images lose 60–80% of their file size with no visible difference to the human eye.
Is my image uploaded to a server when I compress it?
No. ZenithCraft's compressor runs 100% inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas technology. Your image never leaves your device. No cloud upload, no privacy risk — safe for sensitive documents and personal photos.
What image formats can I compress?
You can compress JPG, PNG and WEBP images. All three formats are fully supported with the same quality slider and instant preview.
How do I compress an image for an Australian or Canadian visa photo?
Open the Image Compressor, upload your photo, adjust the quality slider until the file size matches your visa authority's requirement, then click Download. Works for UK, Australian, Canadian, UAE and all international visa photo size requirements. No account needed.
Can I compress images for Shopify or Amazon product listings?
Yes. E-commerce sellers in the UK, USA and worldwide use ZenithCraft to compress product photos before uploading to Shopify, Amazon, Etsy and other platforms — faster page speed, better SEO, no image quality loss.
Is there a file size limit?
ZenithCraft handles most standard image files. Because processing happens in your browser, very large files (above 20MB) may be slower on older devices. For best results, use images under 10MB.
