Image & Media Tools

7 questions
The Image Compressor supports JPG/JPEG, PNG, and WEBP formats. You can also choose to export compressed images in any of these three formats regardless of the original format.
Typically between 50% and 85% reduction depending on the original image content and your chosen quality slider. A 4MB photograph can often be reduced to under 500KB while remaining visually indistinguishable from the original.
The Background Remover works best with solid or near-solid color backgrounds such as white studio backgrounds, plain walls, or flat-color backgrounds. Complex or gradient backgrounds with intricate edges may require an AI-powered dedicated tool for best results.
The Favicon Generator produces 16×16, 32×32, and 64×64 pixel ICO-compatible PNG files. These cover all standard favicon requirements for websites, browser tabs, bookmarks, and home screen shortcuts.
Yes. The JPG/PNG/WEBP Converter handles transparent PNGs. When converting to JPG (which does not support transparency), the transparent areas are filled with white by default.
Base64 encoding converts an image into a text string that can be directly embedded inside HTML, CSS, or JavaScript without needing a separate image file. This is useful for email templates, inline SVG, and reducing HTTP requests in web projects.
There is no imposed file size limit from our side. The practical limit is your browser and device's available memory. Modern browsers handle images up to 20–30MB without issues on most devices with 4GB+ RAM.

Text & Writing Tools

6 questions
The Plagiarism Checker compares sentences within your pasted text to identify repeated or near-duplicate phrases. It calculates an originality score based on sentence uniqueness. It is a local comparison tool — it does not scan the internet — making it ideal for checking self-plagiarism, duplicate sections in reports, or repeated phrasing in essays.
The Readability Checker uses the Flesch-Kincaid Reading Ease and Grade Level formulas — the same system used by the US Department of Defense and academic publishers. A score of 60–70 is considered standard (readable by most adults). A higher score means easier reading; lower means more complex.
The converter supports: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, and CONSTANT_CASE. All conversions happen instantly as you type.
Yes. The Word Counter shows word count, total characters (with spaces), characters without spaces, sentence count, paragraph count, and estimated reading time — all updated in real time as you type or paste.
The Hashtag Generator uses a curated database of high-performing hashtags organized by topic, niche, and engagement tier. It does not connect to live social media APIs, so it generates consistently relevant hashtags based on your keyword input rather than real-time trends.
It removes all Unicode emoji characters, emoticons, and decorative symbols from text while preserving all standard Latin letters, numbers, punctuation, and line breaks. Useful for cleaning social media copy before pasting into formal documents or databases.
